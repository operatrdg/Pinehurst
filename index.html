Itinerary overview

Four days in the Sandhills, built around the Malbon Buckets Cup at Pinehurst No. 4.

Cool mornings, long shadows and some of the best golf in the country. Tuesday we settle in, Wednesday we play the Buckets Cup at No. 4, Thursday we take on Tobacco Road, and then we put our feet up at The Umstead.
